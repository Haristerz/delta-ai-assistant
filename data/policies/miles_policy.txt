DELTA AIR LINES - SKYMILES PROGRAM POLICY

EARNING MILES
Delta SkyMiles members earn miles based on the fare class purchased and their Medallion status.
Base miles are calculated on the distance flown multiplied by a fare class bonus multiplier.
Silver Medallion members earn 7 miles per dollar spent on Delta flights.
Gold Medallion members earn 8 miles per dollar spent on Delta flights.
Platinum Medallion members earn 9 miles per dollar spent on Delta flights.
Diamond Medallion members earn 11 miles per dollar spent on Delta flights.
Non-Medallion members earn 5 miles per dollar spent on Delta flights.
Miles are also earned on partner airlines including Air France, KLM, and Virgin Atlantic.
Credit card purchases on the Delta SkyMiles credit card earn 2 miles per dollar on Delta purchases and 1 mile per dollar on all other purchases.

REDEEMING MILES
Miles can be redeemed for award flights, seat upgrades, companion certificates, and travel experiences.
Award flight redemptions start at 5,000 miles for short domestic routes.
Transatlantic award flights start at 30,000 miles one way in economy class.
First class transatlantic award flights start at 75,000 miles one way.
Miles can be used to cover taxes and fees on award tickets.
Miles do not expire as long as the account has activity within 24 months.
Members can transfer miles to other SkyMiles accounts for a fee of 1 cent per mile transferred.

MILES EXPIRATION
SkyMiles miles never expire as long as there is qualifying account activity every 24 months.
Qualifying activity includes earning or redeeming miles, credit card purchases, or partner transactions.
Inactive accounts with no activity for 24 consecutive months will have all miles forfeited.
Members will receive an email warning 6 months before miles are set to expire.

MEDALLION STATUS REQUIREMENTS
Silver Medallion: 25,000 Medallion Qualifying Miles (MQM) or 30 Medallion Qualifying Segments (MQS) per calendar year.
Gold Medallion: 50,000 MQM or 60 MQS per calendar year.
Platinum Medallion: 75,000 MQM or 100 MQS per calendar year.
Diamond Medallion: 125,000 MQM per calendar year.
Medallion Qualifying Dollars (MQD) waiver available for SkyMiles credit card holders spending $25,000 annually.
Status is valid from the date earned through January 31 of the following year.
