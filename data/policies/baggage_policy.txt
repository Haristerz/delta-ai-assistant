DELTA AIR LINES - BAGGAGE POLICY

CARRY-ON BAGGAGE
Each passenger is allowed one carry-on bag and one personal item at no charge.
Carry-on bag maximum dimensions are 22 x 14 x 9 inches including handles and wheels.
Personal items such as purses, laptop bags, or small backpacks must fit under the seat in front of you.
Personal item maximum dimensions are 18 x 14 x 8 inches.
Overhead bin space is limited and bags may need to be checked at the gate if bins are full.
Gate-checked bags are returned at the jet bridge upon arrival at no charge.

CHECKED BAGGAGE FEES
First checked bag fee for domestic flights: $30 per bag.
Second checked bag fee for domestic flights: $40 per bag.
Third and additional checked bags: $150 per bag each way.
International flights to most destinations: first bag free for economy class.
Oversized bags (over 62 linear inches): additional $200 fee per bag.
Overweight bags (51-70 pounds): additional $100 fee per bag.
Overweight bags (71-100 pounds): additional $200 fee per bag.
Bags over 100 pounds are not accepted as checked baggage.

BAGGAGE ALLOWANCE BY MEDALLION STATUS
Silver Medallion members: first checked bag free on domestic flights.
Gold Medallion members: first and second checked bags free on domestic and most international flights.
Platinum Medallion members: first and second checked bags free on all flights.
Diamond Medallion members: first three checked bags free on all flights.
Delta SkyMiles Reserve and Platinum credit card holders: first checked bag free for cardholder and up to 8 companions on the same reservation.

SPECIAL ITEMS
Sporting equipment such as golf bags, skis, and bicycles are accepted with applicable fees.
Golf bags and ski equipment: $30 fee each way on domestic flights.
Bicycles must be disassembled and in a hard-sided case: $30 fee each way.
Musical instruments that can fit in overhead bins are accepted as carry-on at no charge.
Firearms must be declared at check-in, unloaded, and in a locked hard-sided container.

LOST OR DAMAGED BAGGAGE
Delta is liable for up to $3,800 for lost or damaged baggage on domestic flights.
International baggage liability is governed by the Montreal Convention.
Baggage claims must be filed within 24 hours for damaged bags and within 21 days for delayed bags.
Delayed baggage will be delivered to your address at no charge once located.
Members can track baggage status using the Fly Delta app or at delta.com.
