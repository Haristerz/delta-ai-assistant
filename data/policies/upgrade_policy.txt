DELTA AIR LINES - UPGRADE POLICY

COMPLIMENTARY UPGRADES FOR MEDALLION MEMBERS
Delta Medallion members are eligible for complimentary upgrades to First Class and Delta Comfort+ on domestic flights.
Upgrades are confirmed based on availability and Medallion status level.
Diamond Medallion members receive upgrade priority followed by Platinum, Gold, and Silver members.
Complimentary upgrades are not available on Basic Economy fares.
Upgrades are automatically requested at time of ticketing for eligible members.

UPGRADE WAITLIST
Members are placed on the upgrade waitlist at the time of booking.
Waitlist position is determined by Medallion status, then fare class, then time of check-in.
Upgrades from the waitlist are processed starting 24 hours before departure.
Members can check their waitlist position in the Fly Delta app or at delta.com.
If upgraded, members receive a notification via email or the Fly Delta app.

GLOBAL UPGRADE CERTIFICATES
Platinum and Diamond Medallion members receive Global Upgrade Certificates each year.
Global Upgrade Certificates can be used for upgrades to Delta One (business class) on international flights.
Platinum Medallion members receive 4 Global Upgrade Certificates annually.
Diamond Medallion members receive 8 Global Upgrade Certificates annually.
Certificates must be applied at least 24 hours before departure.
Certificates can be used for the member and one companion traveling on the same itinerary.
Unused certificates expire on January 31 each year.

MILES UPGRADES
Members can use SkyMiles to upgrade to a higher cabin class.
Domestic upgrades using miles start at 5,000 miles each way.
International upgrades to Delta One start at 30,000 miles each way.
Miles upgrades are subject to availability and must be requested at least 24 hours before departure.
Miles upgrades can be combined with upgrade certificates for longer itineraries.

DELTA COMFORT+ UPGRADES
Delta Comfort+ offers extra legroom, priority boarding, and dedicated overhead bin space.
Comfort+ seats can be purchased at time of booking for a fee ranging from $15 to $80 depending on route.
Silver and higher Medallion members can upgrade to Comfort+ for free based on availability.
Comfort+ upgrades for Medallion members are confirmed at check-in if not confirmed earlier.
Comfort+ is available on most domestic and select international flights.

SAME DAY UPGRADES
Same day confirmed upgrades are available for a fee of $75 for domestic flights.
Same day standby upgrades are free for Medallion members and $75 for non-Medallion members.
Same day upgrades can be requested starting at midnight on the day of travel.
Same day upgrade requests can be made through the Fly Delta app, delta.com, or at the airport kiosk.
